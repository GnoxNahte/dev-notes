## Texture Coordinates

### Terms

Texture coords: [[#UV]] & [[#ST]] - \[0 - 1]
Texel coords: pixel coords

### UV

Used in DirectX
uv starts from top left, v goes down

### ST

Used in OpenGL
st starts from bottom left, t goes up
