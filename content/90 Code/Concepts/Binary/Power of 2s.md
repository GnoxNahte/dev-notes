## TIP - Quickly find power of 2 manually

From $2^{10}=1024$,
To find $2^{20}$,

$$
\begin{align}
2^{20} &= {(2^{10})}^2 \\
&=1024^2 \\
&=(1000+24)^2 \\
&=1000^2+2(1000)(24)+24^2 \\
&=1,000,000 + 48,000 + 576 \\
&=1,048,576
\end{align}
$$

> [!NOTE] Power of 10s are usually near to 1000, 1,000,000, etc
