## Linear equation

### Proof - Find linear equation using $\vec{n}$ and 1 point

Let $\vec{n}=\begin{bmatrix}a\\b\end{bmatrix}$, $P=(x_{0},y_{0})$ and $Q=(x,y)$.

> [!NOTE] P and Q representation
> P is a point we know, Q represents the point the equation solves

$$
\begin{align}
\vec{PQ}\perp \vec{n}&=0 \\
\vec{PQ}\cdot\vec{n}&=0 \\
\begin{bmatrix}x-x_{0}\\y-y_{0}\end{bmatrix}\cdot \begin{bmatrix}a\\b\end{bmatrix}&=0  \\
ax-ax_{0}+by-by_{0}&=0 \\
ax+by&=ax_{0}+by_{0}
\end{align}
$$
