## Syntax
$$
\vec{v}=\begin{bmatrix}
x \\
y \\
z
\end{bmatrix}
$$
- $\vec{PQ} = Q - P$ = Vector from Point P -> Point Q (*Destination - Source*)
- $\vec{0}$ = $\begin{bmatrix}0 \\ 0\end{bmatrix}$

### Alt notation
$\vec{v}=i+j+k$
- $i$ = x component
- $j$ = y-component
- $k$ = z-component
Example:
- $\vec{v}=3i+4j+5k=\begin{bmatrix}3x \\ 4y \\ 5z\end{bmatrix}$

## Info
### Addition
![[90 Math/03 Vectors/Drawings/Vector 2D - Drawings.excalidraw#^clippedframe=sqfb5aByBXBhqMqAu9xsD]]
Addition chains the vectors. 
Example:
- $\vec{v_{1}}+\vec{v_{2}}$= start point of $v_{1}$ to endpoint of $v_2$
- $\vec{PR}=\vec{PQ}+\vec{QR}$
### Subtraction
![[90 Math/03 Vectors/Drawings/Vector 2D - Drawings.excalidraw#^clippedframe=lXIXuajBPu0_2mMoh8bWa\|1000]]
**To remember**: Destination - Source

Example:
- $\vec{v_{1}}-\vec{v_{2}}$= endpoint of $v_{2}$ to endpoint of $v_1$
- $\vec{RQ}=\vec{PQ}-\vec{PR}$

Another way to see it:
- $\vec{PQ}=\vec{PR}+\vec{RQ}\implies \vec{RQ}=\vec{PQ}-\vec{PR}$

### Vector projection derivation
Link: [Interactive](https://www.geogebra.org/m/XShfg9r8)
![[90 Math/03 Vectors/Drawings/Vector 2D - Drawings.excalidraw#^clippedframe=QhfDdQCMkbm5IkesfQVK3\|600]]
$\vec{PS}$ is a scalar on $\vec{PR}$, so $\vec{PS} = c\times\hat{PR}$ (constant $\times$ $\vec{PR}$ unit vector)
So to find $c$ (which is $\|\vec{PS}\|$),

$$
\begin{align}
\cos{\theta} &= \frac{\|\vec{PS}\|}{\|\vec{PQ}\|} \\
\|\vec{PS}\| &= \|\vec{PQ}\|\cos{\theta} \\
 \text{Substitute} \cos{\theta} &\text{ with find angle with dot product equation} \left( \cos{\theta} =\frac{\vec{a}\cdot\vec{b}}{|\vec{a}\|\|\vec{b}\|} \right)  \\
\|\vec{PS}\|&=\|\vec{PQ}\|\left( \frac{\vec{PS}\cdot\vec{PQ}}{\|\vec{PS}\|\|\vec{PQ}\|} \right) \\
\|\vec{PS}\|&=\frac{\vec{PS}\cdot\vec{PQ}}{\|\vec{PS}\|}\\
\|\vec{PS}\|&=\vec{PQ}\cdot\hat{PS} \\
\text{So, }\vec{PS}&=(\vec{PQ}\cdot\hat{PS})\hat{PS} 
\end{align}

$$
