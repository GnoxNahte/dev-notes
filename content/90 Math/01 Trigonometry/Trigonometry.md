## Basics
$$\sin{\theta}=\frac{opposite}{hypotenuse} $$
$$\cos{\theta}=\frac{adjacent}{hypotenuse}$$
$$\tan{\theta}=\frac{opposite}{adjacent}=\frac{\sin{\theta}}{\cos{\theta}}$$
$$\sin^2{\theta} + \cos^2{\theta} = 1$$

## For **non-right angled** triangles
### Law of Sines
$$\frac{\sin{\alpha}}{a}=\frac{\sin{\beta}}{b}=\frac{\sin{\gamma}}{c}$$
### Law of Cosines
$$a^2=b^2+C^2-2bc\cos{\alpha}$$

## Compound Angles
$$\sin{(\alpha\pm\beta)}=\sin{\alpha}\cos{\beta}\pm \cos{\alpha}\sin{\beta}$$
$$\cos{(\alpha\pm\beta)}=\cos{\alpha}\cos{\beta}\pm \sin{\alpha}\sin{\beta}$$
$$\tan{(\alpha\pm\beta)}=\frac{\tan{\alpha}\pm \tan{\beta}}{1\pm \tan{\alpha}\tan{\beta}}$$
## Double Angles
$$
\sin{2\alpha}=2\sin{\alpha}\cos{\alpha}
$$

$$
\begin{align}
\cos{2\alpha}&=\cos^2{\alpha}-\sin^2{\alpha} \\
&=2\cos^2{\theta}-1 \\
&=1-2\sin^2{\theta}
\end{align}
$$

$$\tan{2\alpha}=\frac{2\tan{\alpha}}{1-\tan^2{\alpha}}$$

## R Formula
When there is an expression involving sum of sine and cosine terms:
$$a\sin{\theta}\pm b\cos{\theta}=R\sin{(\theta\pm\alpha)}$$
Where $a, b, R$ and $\alpha$ are +ve constants,
$$R=\sqrt{ a^2+b^2 }$$
$$\alpha=\tan^{-1}{\frac{b}{a}}$$

## Converting Radians and Degrees
### Rad $\to$ Degree
$\pi \ rad = 180^{\circ}$
$1\ rad = \frac{180}{\pi}$

$x \ rad = \left( x \times \frac{180}{\pi} \right)^{\circ}$

### Degree $\to$ Rad
$90^{\circ}=\frac{\pi}{2} \ rad$
$180^{\circ} = \pi \ rad$
$270^{\circ}=\frac{3\pi}{2} \ rad$
$360^{\circ} = 2\pi \ rad$

$x^{\circ}=x\times \frac{\pi}{180}\ rad$

## Others
### Reciprocals
$$\csc{\theta} = cosec\ {\theta}=\frac{1}{\sin{\theta}}$$
$$\sec{\theta}=\frac{1}{\cos{\theta}}$$
$$\cot{\theta}=\frac{1}{\tan{\theta}}$$
