## Definition
Adds 1 more coordinate to the matrix/vector/point. 
Using [[90 Math/04 Matrix/3. Affine Matrices\|Affine Matrix]], add 1 more row below
$$
A = \begin{bmatrix}
a & b & c & x \\
d & e & f & y \\
g & h & i & z \\
0 & 0 & 0 & 1
\end{bmatrix}
$$
