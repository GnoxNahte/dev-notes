## Definition

Adds 1 more coordinate to the matrix/vector/point.
Using [[3. Affine Matrices|Affine Matrix]], add 1 more row below

$$
A = \begin{bmatrix}
a & b & c & x \\
d & e & f & y \\
g & h & i & z \\
0 & 0 & 0 & 1
\end{bmatrix}
$$

### Splitting the matrix

alias: decomposing the matrix

Let

$$
A = \begin{bmatrix}
a & b & c & x \\
d & e & f & y \\
g & h & i & z \\
0 & 0 & 0 & 1
\end{bmatrix}
= \begin{bmatrix} v_{1} & v_{2} & v_{3} & v_{4} \end{bmatrix}
$$

Where $v$ (1-4) is the $A$ matrix split into individual columns.

$$
v_{1}=\begin{bmatrix} a \\d \\g \end{bmatrix},
v_{2}=\begin{bmatrix} b \\e \\h \end{bmatrix},
v_{3}=\begin{bmatrix} d \\f \\i \end{bmatrix},
v_{4}=\begin{bmatrix} x \\y \\z \end{bmatrix}
$$

What each column represents:

- $v_{1}$ - what happens to the _x-axis_ ($\begin{bmatrix} 1 \\0 \\0\end{bmatrix}$) after the transformation.
- $v_{2}$ - what happens to the _y-axis_ ($\begin{bmatrix} 0 \\1 \\0\end{bmatrix}$) after the transformation.
- $v_{3}$ - what happens to the _z-axis_ ($\begin{bmatrix} 0 \\0 \\1\end{bmatrix}$) after the transformation.
- $v_{4}$ - amount that is translated.

For example, for $v_{1}$, multiply A with the basis x-axis vector,

$$
A \cdot \begin{bmatrix}1 \\ 0\\ 0\\ 0\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}
a & b & c & x \\
d & e & f & y \\
g & h & i & z \\
0 & 0 & 0 & 1
\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix}1 \\ 0\\ 0\\ 0\end{bmatrix}
=\begin{bmatrix}a\\d\\g\\0\end{bmatrix}
$$
