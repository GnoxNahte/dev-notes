## Quadratic Equation/Factorisation

$x= \frac{-b\pm \sqrt{ b^2-4ac }}{2a}$

### Finding the terms **without** a calculator

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r7DtjXPhmxg)

Given $x^2 + bx + c$, simplify it
Since,
$(x-d)(x-e)=x^2+x(d+e)+de$

To find $d$ and $e$, need to find where

$$
d + e=b\ \ \  \text{\&\&}\ \ \  de=c
$$

So,

1. find factors of the last term ($ab$)
2. Check if those 2 factors can be added together to give ($a+b$)

> [!EXAMPLE]
> $x^2+7x+12$
> Factors of 12
>
> | Factor (a) | Factor (b) | Add result (a+b) |
> | ---------- | ---------- | ---------- |
> | 1          | 12         | 13         |
> | 2          | 6          | 8          |
> | 3          | 4          | 7          |
> Since $3+4==7$ which is the middle term ($7x$),
> $a=3, b=4$
